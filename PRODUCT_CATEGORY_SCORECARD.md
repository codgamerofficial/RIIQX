## 2. Strategic Rationale & Initial Launch Selection

### 🎯 Primary Launch Niche: **Desk & Everyday Organisation (RIIQX Objects) + Home & Living (RIIQX Living)**

1. **Why Desk & Everyday Living Won:**
   - **Zero Fit/Size Confusion:** Unlike apparel where 25-35% of orders are returned due to size and fit issues, desk accessories and lifestyle objects have almost zero size-related returns (RTO < 5%).
   - **Exceptional Net Margins:** Desk mats, solid wood organizers, brass trays, and stone coasters wholesale for ₹350 – ₹850 and retail profitably at ₹1,299 – ₹2,899, yielding **52% to 65% contribution margins**.
   - **Compact Shipping & Low Breakage:** Compact volumetric weight (<1.2 kg), fits standard express courier satchels (BlueDart / Delhivery / Ekart), minimizing freight surcharges.
   - **High Gifting Appeal:** In India, premium desk and home objects have year-round corporate and personal gifting velocity.

### 🎒 Complementary Secondary Collection: **RIIQX Carry & RIIQX Everyday (POD)**
- 5 curated canvas/vegan leather tech sleeves and travel pouches.
- 5 statement heavyweight organic cotton graphic tees (240 GSM) with archival Indian typography produced on-demand via Qikink/Printrove to ensure zero dead inventory.

**Initial Approved Launch SKU Target:** **18 to 24 rigorously verified, high-margin products.**
