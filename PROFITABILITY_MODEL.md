# RIIQX 2.0 — Profitability Model & Unit Economics

**Strict Rule:** Gross merchandise value (GMV) or topline revenue is never described as profit.  
**Contribution Margin Formula:**  
$$\text{Net Contribution} = \text{Selling Price (SP)} - \text{COGS} - \text{Shipping} - \text{Packaging} - \text{Gateway Fee} - \text{GST} - \text{RTO/Return Reserve}$$

---

## 2. Strategic Margin Insights
1. **Hard Goods (Brass & Teak Desk Objects) yield 51%–53% contribution margins** with a break-even ROAS requirement of under 2.0x.
2. **Apparel (POD Tees) yields 35.4% contribution margin** due to the higher return/exchange reserve (10%), reinforcing our Phase 1 decision to anchor the brand around **RIIQX Objects & Living** first, using apparel purely as a brand-building cultural statement.
3. **No product will ever be published below a minimum 30% contribution-margin threshold.**
