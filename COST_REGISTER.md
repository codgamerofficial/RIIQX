## 3. Critical Cash-Flow Invariants & Risk Controls
> [!CAUTION]
> **The Prepayment Gap Trap:** If an e-commerce brand offers Cash on Delivery (COD), it pays the supplier on Day 1 to ship, and couriers take 7–14 days to remit COD cash back to the merchant. With a 25% RTO rate, a ₹0 budget business becomes immediately bankrupt.

### Mandatory Rules:
1. **100% Prepaid Launch:** No COD enabled in Phase 1. Customers pay via UPI, Cards, or Net Banking.
2. **Instant Payment Gateway Webhook Verification:** The customer’s payment must be captured and settled before dispatch orders are issued to the supplier.
3. **5% Contingency Escrow:** For every ₹1,000 in GMV, ₹50 is locked into a reverse-logistics buffer to handle legitimate return shipping without eating into operating capital.
