# RIIQX 2.0 — Statutory & Legal Compliance Manual (India)

**Applicable Legal Framework:**
1. Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 (and subsequent amendments)
2. Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011
3. Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021
4. Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023
5. Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017 & Integrated GST (IGST) Act, 2017

---

## 1. Mandatory E-Commerce Marketplace & D2C Disclosures

Under Rule 6 of the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, every D2C storefront operating in India must display the following information prominently on the product display page (PDP):

### 1.1 Country of Origin
* **Rule:** Explicit country of origin declaration on every product page prior to purchase.
* **RIIQX Implementation:** Displayed as an immutable badge on every card (`Origin: India`) and detailed in the product metadata (`Moradabad, Uttar Pradesh`, `Saharanpur, Uttar Pradesh`, `Jodhpur, Rajasthan`, `Khurja, Uttar Pradesh`).

### 1.2 Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules Disclosures
Every single SKU published in RIIQX 2.0 must state:
1. **Name and Address of Manufacturer / Packer:** Registered artisanal guild or verified POD partner.
2. **Net Quantity:** E.g., `1 N (Valet Tray)`, `1 N (Ceramic Mug)`.
3. **Dimensions / Capacity:** In standard metric units (e.g., `22cm × 14cm × 2.5cm`, `380ml`).
4. **Consumer Care Details:** Name, email, phone number, and physical postal address for customer inquiries.
5. **Maximum Retail Price (MRP):** Inclusive of all taxes (`₹1,899 incl. of all taxes`).

## 2. Statutory Nodal & Grievance Redressal Architecture

Under Rule 5(6) of the E-Commerce Rules, RIIQX must publish an official Grievance Redressal mechanism:

| Role | Statutory Requirement | RIIQX Designation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grievance Officer** | Name, telephone number, and email address published on website | Appointed & listed on `/about` and `/contact` |
| **Acknowledgment SLA** | Redressal mechanism must acknowledge customer complaint within 48 hours | Automated ticketing webhook (`/api/support/ticket`) |
| **Resolution SLA** | Redressal must resolve customer complaint within 1 month from date of receipt | 7 business days internal resolution target |
| **Nodal Person of Contact** | 24×7 coordination with law enforcement and government agencies | Registered compliance director |

### 3.1 Automated Tax Invoice Requirements
Every order generated by RIIQX Commerce OS automatically produces a GST-compliant tax invoice containing:
* Supplier / RIIQX Legal Entity GSTIN
* Customer Billing & Shipping Address with State Code
* Sequential Invoice Number (e.g. `INV/2026-27/00142`)
* HSN/SAC Code per line item
* Segregated Tax Breakdown (CGST, SGST, IGST)
* Digital QR code for UPI / E-Invoice verification

## 4. Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act Compliance

1. **Consent Architecture:** Explicit customer consent captured during checkout for order fulfillment, courier coordination, and WhatsApp status updates.
2. **Data Minimization:** No biometric data, sensitive financial credentials, or full card numbers stored on RIIQX servers; all card data tokenized via RBI-licensed payment aggregators (Razorpay / Cashfree).
3. **Right to Erasure:** Customer account settings provide an instant "Delete Account & Personal Data" tool under [`RiiqxSettingsView.tsx`](file:///d:/GlowFit%20AI/src/components/riiqx/RiiqxSettingsView.tsx).
